    How is age calculated?

    The calculator subtracts the date of birth from the chosen date, year by year, month by month, then day by day. If the day of the month is smaller than the birth day, it borrows the days of the previous month, the same way you would borrow in normal subtraction.

    Age on a past or future date

    Change "Age at date" to find how old someone was, or will be, on any day, for example on a job application deadline or an exam date.
