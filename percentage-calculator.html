    Percentage formulas

    X% of Y = X / 100 x Y. X is what percent of Y = X / Y x 100. Percentage change = (new - old) / old x 100. A positive change is an increase, a negative one is a decrease.

    Examples

    20% of 150 is 30. 30 is 20% of 150. A price going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase, and from 100 to 80 is a 20% decrease.
