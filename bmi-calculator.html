    How is BMI calculated?

    BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)². For example, 65 kg at 1.65 m gives 65 / 2.72 = 23.9, which is in the normal range.

    BMI categories

    Under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 is normal, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or more is obese. BMI is a screening tool and does not measure body fat directly, so talk to a doctor for personal health advice.
